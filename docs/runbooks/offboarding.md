# Offboarding Runbook — Arca Cert - Certification Dossier Builder

1. Revoke the leaver's OIDC identities and Vault roles (SOCResponder.revoke).
2. Rotate any shared credentials the person could have accessed.
3. Reassign open incidents, reviews and owned ADRs.
4. Audit the last 90 days of their actions via the audit trail.
5. Confirm CODEOWNERS and on-call rotations are updated.
